# Notes and Events

Now the most interesting is here! Notes! So we're gonna use SuperNova for showing the notes.

## Note list

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

As we can see in the image provided above, there's Select button, Tap note button, Hold note button, Flick button, Hold Tick button, Trace button, Guide button, Time button, SV (or simply Speed) button and Event button!

{% hint style="info" %}
To get better note placement you can change Divisions via using your keys W and Q. Q is to reduce divisions (e.g. from 64 to 32 divisions), W is to increase divisions (e.g. from 32 to 64 divisions). Maximum divisions is 1920.
{% endhint %}

## Event List

Now we move on to the interesting part! Events! In here it include: BPM Changes, Time Signature Changes, Hi-Speed Changes, And Dynamic Stages!

### 00:00 (BPM and Time Signature)

With this tool you can change BPM and Time Signature on the place you need to sync your chart!

### SV (Hi-Speed)

With this one you can change the note speed in your chart to make it fun to play! (Or make it hard to play)

As you can see in the screenshot above, there's many Events that are available, let's check them out!

#### Fever

Adding a Fever makes your chart have a Fever bar at the certain period of chart (e.g. fever starts at 1:29 and ends at 1:43). Do not increase your score after you get Fever/Super Fever in Sonolus.

#### Skill

Adding a Skill makes your chart to have Skill animation. Do not increase your life/score/accuracy in Sonolu.

{% hint style="info" %}
Fever and Skill events are mostly to make Sonolus chart recording look like Project Sekai.
{% endhint %}
